Questions

Does MasterLoop make sound?

No. MasterLoop is a MIDI generator and contains no synthesizer. It sends MIDI to the instruments you already own, so every loop uses your own sounds.

Which DAWs and formats are supported?

MasterLoop is a VST3 plug-in for Windows and macOS 11 or later. It works in Ableton Live, Reaper, Bitwig Studio, Studio One and other VST3 hosts. Hosts that load only Audio Units, such as Logic Pro, cannot load it.

How do I use it in Ableton Live?

Use Hub + Parts mode. One MasterLoop instance composes, and up to four small Part instances each send one layer to its own instrument. The setup takes four steps and is covered in the manual.

Can I drag the result into my project?

Yes. Drag any layer onto a DAW track as a MIDI clip, or export the whole loop as a .mid file with one track per layer.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. A full-featured 7-day trial starts only when you press the button inside the plug-in. Scanning plug-ins never starts it.

How many computers can I use?

One licence key activates on up to two of your computers. Deactivate an old machine in Settings to free the slot.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It needs the internet to activate and for an occasional licence refresh, roughly monthly. Your music and MIDI never leave your computer.
