How it works

Detect. Warp. Rebound.

KINETIC treats a kick or a bass note as mass, and lets physics decide how the sound moves.

Detect

Mass detector

Level-independent within ±12 dB. Hit mode uses a transient detector; Follow mode reacts continuously to one frequency band so no note is missed.

Warp

Micro-Doppler delay

A 2–15 ms fractional delay with Hermite interpolation bends pitch and time, never level. Infall pitches up, Outfall pitches down.

Rebound

Spring-mass return

An inertial spring brings the sound back with natural overshoot. Orbit sets the speed, Elasticity the bounce.

Features

Built for serious low-end work

Target

Event Horizon

Choose which part of the spectrum falls into the well: Low, High or Band, with 12 or 24 dB/oct slopes. The untouched band stays phase-aligned.

Track

Auto-Track

A 1/3-octave band bank finds the dominant input frequency and can move the Event Horizon with it. Threshold, speed and hold are adjustable.

Sidechain

Kick drives bass

Feed a kick into the optional sidechain and process the bass. The plug-in shows whether your host really sends audio, with fallback to the main input.

Align

Phase Align

Measures kick-to-bass phase at the tracked frequency and shifts the bass by a pure time offset: Quadrature, Constructive or Custom angle. No EQ, no gain change.

Stereo

Gravity Width & Polarity

Let the left and right springs drift apart during the rebound, and flip between Infall and Outfall.

Workflow

Presets & A/B

Factory presets by category, portable user presets, A/B comparison with copy, and one-click save.

Made for EDM, bass music, hip-hop, trap and techno: kick/bass interaction, sub movement, bass design and sound design.

FAQ

Questions

Is it a compressor or sidechain ducker?

No. KINETIC works with micro time-warping and elastic rebound. The level of your signal is never reduced.

Does it add latency?

Yes, a fixed 18 ms. Your DAW's plug-in delay compensation takes care of it.

Can I use it offline?

Yes, after activation. The plug-in renews its license silently when you are online. After a long offline period it asks you to reconnect once.

I bought a new computer. What now?

Deactivate the old device inside the plug-in and activate the new one, or email support and we will free a slot.

Which data does the plug-in send?

Only your license key, an anonymous machine hash and the plug-in version, to our activation server. No tracking, no analytics.
