Privacy Policy

Crafted Hertz · Last updated October 3, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Crafted Hertz is responsible for the data described below, except for payment data, where Creem is the merchant of record and acts under its own privacy policy.

2. This website

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics on this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. The Buy button loads a checkout provided by Creem.

3. Plug-in activation data

To start the trial, activate, renew or deactivate a license, the plug-in sends our activation server: your license key (or a hash of it), an anonymous hash of your machine identifier, the plug-in version and the time of the request. We use this only to enforce the trial and license, prevent abuse and give you support. We do not collect audio, project data, plug-in settings or usage analytics.

4. Purchases

When you buy, Creem collects your name, email, billing and payment details and provides us with the information needed to deliver your license and give support. See Creem's privacy policy for how it handles that data.

5. Support emails

If you write to us, we process your email address and message to answer you, and keep the conversation as long as needed for support and legal reasons.

6. Why we process data

To perform our contract with you (delivering and licensing the Software), to protect against license abuse (legitimate interest), to comply with law, and to answer your requests.

7. Sharing and retention

We share data only with service providers that operate our activation server and hosting, and with Creem for payments. We do not sell personal data. Activation records are kept while your license is active and for a limited period afterwards to prevent trial and license abuse.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to processing. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The Software and website are not directed at children under 16.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
