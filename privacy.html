PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

At Crafted Hertz, accessible from www.craftedhertz.com, your privacy is of paramount importance to us as an independent audio software developer. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Crafted Hertz and how we use it.

E-Commerce & Software Licensing

To provide a secure and reliable checkout experience, we partner with Lemon Squeezy, which acts as our Merchant of Record. When you purchase a license for Resynth or any of our software products, your payment and billing information is processed entirely by Lemon Squeezy. Crafted Hertz does not store or process your credit card details. We only receive the necessary information (such as your name and email address) required to issue your software license keys, manage your account, and provide technical support.

Plugin Usage & Telemetry

We respect your creative environment. Our VST3/AU plugins (such as Resynth) do not collect or transmit your personal audio files, DAW session data, or sensitive local files. The plugin only requires an internet connection during the initial activation of your license or during the 7-day trial verification, where it communicates with the Lemon Squeezy API to validate your key. Once activated, a local encrypted configuration file is stored on your machine to authorize offline use.

Log Files & Website Analytics

Crafted Hertz follows a standard procedure of using log files on our website. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and are used solely for site optimization and technical debugging.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Crafted Hertz uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

If you have additional questions, require support regarding your license, or need more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through the Support form on our main page.
